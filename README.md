# Academic Translate

Translate PDFs into 30+ languages while keeping the original page layout: columns, tables, figures and equations stay where they were.

**https://academictranslate.com**

## What it does

- Translates the text of a PDF and sets it back on the original page, so a two-column paper stays two-column.
- Leaves mathematical formulas untranslated and re-typesets them as LaTeX, so equations and their numbers stay intact.
- Translates table cells in place and keeps figure captions next to their figures.
- Shows the original and the translation side by side for review.
- Delivers a translated PDF and a bilingual PDF (each original page followed by its translation).

## Good fit

- Journal and conference papers (arXiv, IEEE, ACM, Springer, Elsevier, Nature and similar)
- Theses, lecture notes and textbooks with equations, tables and figures
- Multi-column documents that ordinary document translators flatten into a single column

## Pricing

- Free: 30 pages every month for verified accounts, plus 5 welcome pages on first sign-in. Without an account, the first 5 pages of a document are free.
- Credit packs (one-time, never expire): 300 pages for $1.99, 2,000 pages for $5.99.
- 1 credit = 1 translated page. Pages that fail to translate are not charged.
- There is no page limit on a document.
- Uploaded files and results are deleted automatically 24 hours after upload.
